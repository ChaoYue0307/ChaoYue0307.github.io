Notes

Thoughts in progress.

A small living notebook for research taste, engineering habits, and personal reminders.

Evaluation before automation. If we cannot measure it, we cannot improve it. I prefer starting with baselines, error analysis, and careful task design.

Reproducibility is a feature. A result I cannot reproduce is a result I do not yet understand. Configs, seeds, data versions, and notes deserve first-class treatment.

Good research tools lower friction. The best infrastructure makes careful work easier without hiding the assumptions.

Small tools, sharp edges. I like utilities that do one thing clearly, compose well, and stay understandable under pressure.

Interfaces are part of the system. CLIs, logs, docs, and dashboards shape how people reason about the work.

Ship with a path back. Keep changes reviewable, reversible, and easy to validate.

Write to think. Writing turns vague ideas into objects that can be tested, edited, and shared.

Stay playful, build seriously. Curiosity is the fuel; disciplined execution is the practice.

Make the work useful. A good project should help someone else move faster or see more clearly.

Let's connect Open to research conversations, collaboration, and interesting systems problems.
